01 · UNIVERSITY PROJECT

MAKE IT MAD

A six-month campaign proposal for The Mad Alchemy, designed to move students from campus awareness to digital participation, repeat purchase and loyalty.

THE BRIEFIncrease repeat purchase among university students while improving campus brand awareness, social engagement, QR ordering and participation in the existing digital loyalty system.

THE IDEAConvert campus convenience into a repeat-purchase habit: build brand recall, earn attention with student-relevant content, drive QR adoption through Mad Monday and make loyalty points tangible with the Mad Cup.

MY FOCUSTurn student surveys, social listening and on-site observations into four content pillars, a six-month calendar and post-and-reel concepts connected to the loyalty journey.

02 · L’ORÉAL BRANDSTORM · TEAM ENTRY

WEARABLE FRAGRANCE

A team competition concept exploring how fragrance could become a more personal, portable and wearable experience.

THE OPPORTUNITYFast-paced consumers move between work, exercise and social settings, but refreshing fragrance throughout the day is not always convenient or discreet.

THE CONCEPTThe Pulse is a wearable fragrance device with bio-responsive activation, interchangeable scent cartridges and a connected app experience.

PROJECT SCOPEThe proposal brings together audience and market analysis, product design, technology, sustainability and a phased go-to-market strategy.
